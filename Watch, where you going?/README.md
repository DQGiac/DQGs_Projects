## Game development
Made with p5.js, in three days, for GMTK Game Jam 2025.<br>
Had plenty of time, was bored (this was right after the college entrance exam), so decided to try making games again.<br>
The idea is not original at all, and the title is a bad wordplay, but pretty good for 3 hours of brainstorming haha.<br>
Though I am not particularly proud of the graphics and gameplay, this is the first game I experimented with an actual particle system.<br>
Might update this later.
