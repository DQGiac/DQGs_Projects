Bitsy Bits was created in 48 hours (only around 6 hours cumulatively) for _<a href="https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2021">GMTK Game Jam 2021</a>_
- Jam theme: _**Joined Together**_
- Idea: computer bits sticking with other bits to grow and survive the attacks of the red bits
